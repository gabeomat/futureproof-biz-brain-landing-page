How this started

The expensive system that was too complicated to use.

A while back, I paid to get access to a social media content workflow from a very technical AI creator. Smart guy. Genuinely good at what he does. So I built the system exactly the way he laid it out.

And honestly? It was too much. Sub-agents. Connectors. Multiple moving pieces. A whole setup that had to be wired together just right before it would do anything useful. And after all that, the results were underwhelming.

That's what I keep noticing with a lot of the technical AI world. They build impressive systems, but they often make them so complicated that you practically need to become an engineer just to use them.

So I built my own version. No code. No complicated setup. No black box. Just a practical AI social media manager I could run inside Claude, in my own voice, for my own business.

And it worked better. It writes posts that actually sound like me. It creates branded content. It helps me plan a full week of posts. It can connect to my calendar or scheduler. And I still approve everything before anything goes out.

The part that matters I don't want AI posting random off-brand content while I'm asleep. I want a workflow that does the heavy lifting, but keeps me in charge. And that's exactly what we're going to build together.
