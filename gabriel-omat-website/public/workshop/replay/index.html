Living Workspace · Starter Prompt
I want to build a Living Workspace for my business. The purpose of this workspace is to give my AI assistant a clear, organized, always-current understanding of my business so it can help me think, plan, execute, write, make decisions, and track what is changing over time. I want to use a simple four-layer architecture: 1. CLAUDE.md This is the routing file. It should stay lean. It should include who I am, what my business does, key terms I use, my current offers or projects, my preferences, and pointers to deeper documents. It should not become a giant strategy document. 2. TASKS.md This is the execution layer. It should show my current locked goals for the week, what I am actively working on, what is in the backlog, and what needs a decision. This is the first place you should look when I ask, "What am I working on?" 3. KNOWLEDGE/wiki/ This is the rationale layer. This is where the deeper thinking lives. Any important strategy, offer decision, customer insight, positioning idea, funnel strategy, or business reasoning should be stored here as individual wiki-style notes. 4. LOG.md This is the chronology. It should contain dated entries of meaningful changes: what shifted, what got renamed, what got retired, what got replaced, and why. Help me design this for myself. I will provide some key information and you can question me to fill in the gaps.
